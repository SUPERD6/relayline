## Metrics
Catch rate = wrong ones blocked / 15. False-block rate = right ones blocked / 14. Unknown is listed separately, with numerator and denominator. Cost in API-equivalent dollars.

## Verdict (in order)
1. Units actually replayed < 25: **Unknown**; report the reason, do not re-run beyond budget.
2. Worst-case reading (Unknown counts as not caught among the wrong ones, and as a false block among the right ones): catch >= 8/15 and false blocks <= 2/14 -> **Pass**.
3. Best-case reading (Unknown counts as caught among the wrong ones, and as not blocked among the right ones): catch < 8/15 or false blocks > 2/14 -> **Fail**.
4. Otherwise: **Unknown** (the difference comes entirely from Unknown; look into the Unknown causes first, do not treat it as Pass).

The thresholds come from the plan: catch >= 50% (at least 8 of 15), false blocks <= 20% (at most 2 of 14; 3/14 = 21.4% is already over).

## Improvement round
If it does not pass, at most one more round of changes (engineering standards: at most two rounds per item). The second round gets its own pre-registration with the same thresholds and states that these 29 have been seen, so the second round overfits more easily. If neither round passes: `review.mode=tests` stays off by default and the negative result is published.

## Budget and interruption
`--budget 5` hard cap; the plan cap is $5, the 1.5x line is $7.5. On interruption keep all rows; failures and interruptions are both accounted.
