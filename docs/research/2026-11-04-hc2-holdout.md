# hc2: repository-task recheck on the 0.6.0 candidate (2026-11-04)

English · [中文](2026-11-04-hc2-holdout.zh.md) · [Research reports](README.md) · [pg3b receipts](2026-11-03-pg3b-receipts.md) · [Historical hc1](2026-10-29-hc1-holdout.md)

**The lead judged this recheck passed under a proportional reading of an ambiguous preregistration.** Hidden
tests passed 28/30 overall, 25/27 on `env_clean` rows. Cost per success (clean rows) was $0.088 and median wall
time 168 s, versus historical hc1's $0.063 and 61 s. Self and the built-in sub-agent were not rerun. Measured
cost and wall time were higher; code and sandbox environment effects were not isolated.

## Preregistration and its interpretation

Registered on 2026-11-03 before execution: candidate pin `b9d1d54`, isolated clone, 10 repository tasks × 3 rounds,
ccx2 only, $3 hard budget (estimated $3). Hidden tests score correctness; the lead event stream's final receipt
defines its kind. The primary false-positive metric counts hidden-test failures among ordinary `ok` receipts,
excluding `ok_self_checked`. Also report self-checked failures, review failures that were right, spend and Unknown.

The ordered rules were: (1) fewer than 25 clean rows or 10 ordinary `ok` receipts means insufficient evidence;
(2) any false positive stops release; (3) hidden-test successes ≤25/30 means suspected regression, stop release,
26–28 is not regression but must be reported; (4) otherwise pass the recheck and continue release.
The regression metric also says to count only `env_clean` rows. **Rule 3 is ambiguous:** all rows give 28/30;
clean rows give 25/27. Proportionally 25/27 is about 27.8/30 and does not trigger regression, but the literal
number 25 would trigger it. The lead used the proportional interpretation and explicitly records it here.

There were 27 clean rows (≥25), 19 ordinary `ok` receipts (≥10), and zero false positives (17 ordinary `ok`
receipts on clean rows, also zero). Three `env_clean=False` rows had other local ccx activity writing the real
ledger during execution. Overall and clean outcomes are reported separately, not pooled into a clean rate.

## Receipt limits

**Only “no false positives in these 19 ordinary ok receipts” is supported. The approximate 95% upper bound is
15%, not below 2%. Only six had `status=done`.** The pre-#223 audit was 8/209 = 3.8%; there is still no sufficient
post-#223 done-receipt retest. [pg3b](2026-11-03-pg3b-receipts.md) had zero ordinary `ok` receipts and was
insufficient evidence under its preregistration, unable to measure the 2% threshold. Its independent review
passed all 29 self-checked receipts, including 15 hidden-test failures; pass does not mean correct.

The two `undo-not-verdict` receipts (rounds 1 and 3) had an external check fail with `cost_usd=None`, despite
`ok=true`. **Preliminary** inspection suggests an old hold-out test with a hard-coded date (similar to dd88517);
only the commit title and code clues were checked, not a confirmed root cause.

## Cost, time and diagnostic limits

| Measurement | hc1: historical experiment, old code, 2026-10-29 | hc2: 0.6.0 candidate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Hidden tests, all / clean | 29/30 / 29/30 | 28/30 / 25/27 |
| Cost per success, clean | $0.063 | $0.088 |
| Wall time, median | 61 s | 168 s |
| Total cost, all 30 | $1.826 | $2.411 |
| Cumulative wall time, all 30 | 2810 s | 7212 s |

The 30 same-task, same-round pairs show total spend +32% and cumulative wall time +157%. A read-only diagnostic
attributed added cost to active lead $0.309, sub-agent $0.201, review $0.064 and repair $0.011. `ledger-repair`
alone contributed 57% of the added cost and 47% of added time. Routing remained mostly luna/low (38 → 43
entries; only ledger round 3 had one high entry), rather than a general move to expensive models.

21/30 event streams reported pytest temporary-directory permission failures, absent from hc1's streams.
The evaluation enforced an unelevated sandbox after hc1; hc1's sandbox type was not recorded. Claude's version
also changed. The diagnostic did not run pytest; waiting time was estimated by script. Code and environment
contributions remain Unknown. These observations neither establish a product slowdown nor show that savings
against self disappeared: **self and the built-in sub-agent were not rerun** (hc1 self was $0.090, 21 s).
The historical “about 30% cheaper” claim describes hc1 only, not the current version.

The set had been used after hc1 and published: these are no longer wholly unseen tasks. Only one task type and
one lead configuration were tested. Prices are API-equivalent. Spend: pg3 $0.148 + pg3b $1.462 + hc2 $2.411 =
**$4.02 rounded**. No broader correctness or savings claim follows from the release-gate decision.
