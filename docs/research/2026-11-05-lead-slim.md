Each stream's SHA256 was checked against its result row. The repository-safe derived data is
[lead-overhead.json](data/2026-11-05-lead-overhead.json); no personal paths or source sessions are copied there.

The mean lead difference is 14,708 tokens. The 26 candidate tasks that returned `small_self` isolate the
unnecessary round-trip: the first Bash request writes the copied request to `brief.md` and calls `ccx run`, gets a
refusal, then reads/implements the source itself. Their mean difference is 15,353 tokens and 1.115 requests;
the 24 pairs where both arms are clean have 14,803 tokens and 1.083 requests of difference. The other four
candidate units actually delegated; their different execution paths are not treated as small-task overhead.

The command averages 1,659 characters and the refusal reply 483 characters. The ~688 first-context difference
includes prompt and framing differences; it is not a direct tokenizer measurement of one string. Tool definitions
and the ordinary system prompt are shared context, charged again by the extra request above. Do not add them
again. The decision request is counted once; prompt/replay estimates cover subsequent requests only. These
components reconcile the observed totals but are an attribution estimate, not a causal experiment.
Cached input and cache creation have different prices. Reducing total tokens alone does not determine dollars.
The historical all-row verdict remains Fail; two unclean candidate rows are retained, not discarded.

## Implementation

[Product usage](../lead-slim.md). One small module generates the actual small-task guidance for managed blocks,
turn hooks and the new eval arm. It reuses the file/context/quota thresholds, with no extra probe for the shortcut.
Uncertain sizing and required ccx facilities keep the delegation path. Picked tiers and cost sizing keep normal
routing. No command, option, existing JSON meaning, exit code, check, review or escalation is removed or weakened.

`ccx3cand` adds `lead.slim_small=true` to the Stage 3 candidate's three switches and read review mode.
Its short-task branch runs no ccx command and writes no brief. Its delegation branch keeps the same flags,
`--files` estimate, one-call wait/follow-up, verbatim brief, check/review/acceptance and resume behavior.
Instructions are compressed; this is a prompt-and-config comparison, not a settings-only experiment.
At fixed label `L`, exercise-specific HOW text is 2,031 characters / 306 words for ccx2cand and
1,829 characters / 243 words for ccx3cand (self: 21 / 4). The shared task instructions are unchanged.
An absent refusal event in the new arm remains null/Unknown, not an invented self-routing event.

## Cost estimate and reproduction

If the 26 small tasks cost exactly their self pair and four delegated tasks cost what they did historically,
the idealized ratio is **1.1326** before any extra guidance cost. Scale the observed first-context delta by the
new/old exercise-guidance character ratio: about 619 extra context tokens on each self-path request. With the
observed 3.067 self requests and the existing r5 pricing assumptions (cache reads $0.20/M, cache writes
$2.50/M for 5 minutes or $4/M for 1 hour), the illustrative ratio becomes **1.208–1.247**.
That estimate still exceeds 1.2. It assumes unchanged implementation, routing, cache placement, quality and four
delegation costs; it is not a measured result, bound, or promise. New-task difficulty and cache variation may
move it either way. The existing test-count and success-cost thresholds remain unchanged.

The actual dry-run CLI schedules **60 units** (30 per arm) and quotes **$9.60**: self $6.00 plus candidate $3.60,
fitting a $10 budget. Its `ccx3cand?` explicitly reuses historical ccx2 pricing, not the projection above. This
conservative planner was preserved; no real spend or new per-success result exists.
